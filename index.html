Data Visualisation Project

Welcome to CFLens! Welcome to the exciting world of data visualization with CFLens. In this project, we delve into the realms of Codeforces, a premier platform for competitive programming enthusiasts. CFLens offers a unique perspective, transcending the mere lines of code to unveil the intricate patterns, trends, and insights hidden within the vast repository of Codeforces submissions. Harness the power of visualization to unravel the secrets of programming competitions.

Journey of the Top Coders

See the progress of best coders from the start of codeforces till today. This chart used a horizontal bar chart to show the competition between the topmost coders on codeforces and their rating changes over the years. The horizontal axis represents the rating and different bars represent users. Right from the start Tourist from Belarus has been at the top, while Indians have struggled to get into the top standings despite being in large numbers on codeforces today.







Problem Tags distribution across Codeforces

Have you ever wondered how many problems does a particular tag have on Codeforces? This chart shows the distribution of problems across different tags on Codeforces. The donut chart shows the percentage of problems in each tag. The tags are color coded and the user can hover over the chart to see the percentage of problems in each tag. The chart is interactive and the user can click on the buttons to modify the appearance.

Additional buttons are provided to show the distribution of problems in each division. The user can click on the buttons to see the distribution of problems in each division.







Average Problems Solved across different titles

This chart shows the average number of problems solved by users across different titles on Codeforces. User can also see the Average Rating Solved by users across different titles on Codeforces. Colours of the bars are based on the colours provided by Codeforces for different titles.



Rating Comparator of Two Users

This chart shows the rating of two users on Codeforces. The user can select two users from the dropdown and see the rating of the two users over time. The chart is interactive and the user can hover over the chart to see the rating of the two users at a particular time. The chart is color coded and the user can see the rating of the two users at a particular time.



Time Distribution Comparator for Multiple Users

This chart shows enables the users to enter upto 4 Codeforces user handles and the Contest ID, to compare the percentage of time taken to complete each question in that contest. This enables a user to compare times with their friends in a visual way, and identify, which questions they spent more time on. It also enables a problem setter, to compare the time distribution of users at various ratings, to understand the trends in time taken to solve problems.







Distribution of Popularity of Codeforces over the World.

In the graph below, one can see the average and maximum rating of each country and the best institutions of that country by clicking on them. By the use of world map we see the distribution of the sport of the competitive programming on Codeforces with useful metrics like average rating and maximum ratings of countries. This gives a brief about the enthusiasm and spread of competitive programming culture over the world. The world map is made interactive with tooltips on hover to show the values. Also there is a dropdown to select the datatype to show whether average or maximum rating. We have shown the density with the help of sequential shades of blue.

With the help of this one can view and compare various top institutions of a particular country. This can be extremely useful for new college joining students to judge the competitive coding cultures of various top colleges.
